Determination of Native Molecular Weight by Gel Filtration Chromatography

    Step 0 · Introduction

    How gel filtration chromatography separates molecules by size

    Gel filtration chromatography uses porous beads as a molecular sieve. Larger molecules cannot enter the pores, so they move around the beads and elute first. Smaller molecules enter the pores, take a longer path, and elute later.

    • Larger molecules stay outside the beads.
    • Smaller molecules diffuse into the porous beads.
    • More time in the pores means a later elution.
